- Set a timer for **5 minutes** to prepare
- Record yourself for **3 minutes**
- Re-listen and identify exactly ONE gap (e.g., "I paused too long before the conclusion")
### 1. Presentation

- Explain a complex concept in 3 minutes (e.g., how a recommendation algorithm works)
- Persuade your audience to adopt a tool/method (e.g., switch to Notion)
- The 1-slide challenge: pitch an idea using only one slide, no bullet points
- Teach something you recently learned (a programming concept, a psychological bias)
- Present a counterintuitive insight (e.g., "Why working less increases productivity")

### 2. Technical Business Meeting

- Defend a technical decision (e.g., why AWS over Azure)
- Explain a delay without blaming (what happened + solution)
- Summarize a 10-minute discussion in 60 seconds
- Ask a sharp clarifying question
- Push back respectfully with a data-backed concern

### 3. Catch-Up Meeting / Small Talk

- "What are you working on that excites you right now?"
- "I read [article/book] about [topic] recently — have you come across it?"
- "What's the dumbest problem you solved this week?"
- "If you had an extra 2 hours this week, what would you spend it on?"
- "What's a skill you want to learn but haven't started yet?"
